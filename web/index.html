Exploring Murmuration

  • Spin the globe by dragging it. Zoom with the scroll wheel or a pinch.
  • Find a post: click any post in the feed on the left. The globe turns to it and marks it in pink. Click it again or press Esc to let go.
  • Read earlier posts: scroll the feed. New posts wait while your pointer is over it.
  • Open a post on Bluesky: click its card on the globe.
  • Colours: amber means the location is a guess from the post's language; cyan means a country hint from the handle (.jp, .de) or language tag; pink is your selection.
  • Keys: Space pause the spin, F fullscreen, T cards on the globe, L translation on or off (once installed), Esc close or deselect.
  • Translate posts into your language with the optional Translation extra (toolbar, top right).

Murmuration goes back to screensaver mode after 2 minutes without any input.

About

Murmuration is free, open-source software (Apache-2.0) that shows public Bluesky posts as they happen. It isn't affiliated with Bluesky Social PBC. Inspired by the 2007 Twingly Screensaver.
